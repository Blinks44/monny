## 2026-10-18: Readings

- Researched every metric's source on macOS 27 (M1 Max). macOS 27 hides battery temperature and raw capacity from unprivileged apps, and Apple's "Maximum Capacity" comes only from `system_profiler`. Per-process network has no public source and was dropped from SPEC. Temperatures and fans moved to CORE-002. History storage is decided as ring buffers plus a binary plist.
- Built the shared sampler and public-API readers and wired them into the popover and the window KPIs. A sampling pass costs 5.7 ms; free space is refreshed every 30 s because it costs 54 ms. Values match `top`, `vm_stat`, `pmset` and System Information ([verification](../verification/readings-public.md)).
