# Monny 1.0.0

The first public release of Monny, a free and open-source menu bar app that shows what your Mac is doing and keeps its battery healthy while it stays plugged in.

## What you can do

- See CPU, memory, disk, network, cores and battery in one click from the menu bar, or open it with ⌥⌘M. Optionally keep CPU load, CPU temperature or battery level next to the icon.
- Open the dashboard for Overview, CPU, Memory, Disk, Network, Battery and Sensors pages with history from 1 minute to 7 days and the busiest apps.
- Limit charging to 80–100% with the built-in macOS charge limit and switch Optimized Battery Charging, on macOS 26.4 and later.
- Check temperatures, fan speeds, SSD health and power draw where your Mac reports them.
- Get notified when the charge limit is reached, the Mac runs hot, the battery is low, memory pressure is high or an app keeps the CPU busy.
- Optionally see how much of your Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Grok limits is left.
- Use the app in English, Russian, German, French, Spanish or Simplified Chinese.

## Install

1. Download **Monny-1.0.0-52.dmg** below, open it and drag Monny into Applications.
2. Open Monny. Because the app is not notarized by Apple, macOS blocks the first launch. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, click **Open Anyway** next to the message about Monny and confirm. Details are in the [installation guide](https://github.com/Blinks44/monny#install).

You can check the download against the attached **SHA256SUMS.txt**: `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## Good to know

- Monny requires a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later. It has been tested on an M1 Max with macOS 27; macOS 26 has had only a short check, and Intel Macs are not supported.
- The charge limit uses the same setting as System Settings → Battery through a private macOS interface. macOS keeps enforcing it after Monny quits. If the setting cannot be read, Monny does not change it and says so.
- Sensors differ between Mac models. A reading your Mac does not report is shown as unavailable rather than estimated.
- Monny checks GitHub for updates and asks before installing one. Readings stay on your Mac and no analytics are sent. AI limits are off by default; when turned on, Monny asks Anthropic, OpenAI, Cursor and xAI for your limits using each tool's own sign-in.
