# Monny 1.1.0

Process search and quitting, a clearer view of power use and two more AI tools.

## What's new

- **Find and quit processes.** Search the Processes tab by app or process name. Right-click a row to Quit it, or Force Quit it after a confirmation. Monny itself and core system processes cannot be ended from here.
- **Power at a glance.** The Battery tab shows how much power the Mac uses, how much goes into charging and the adapter's rating; on battery it shows the draw and the time left. Overview shows the Mac's power draw next to the battery level.
- **JetBrains AI limits.** The monthly AI quota and top-up credits from your JetBrains IDE, read from the IDE's local files without any network request.
- **GitHub Copilot limits.** Premium requests, chat and completions, using the account you signed in to with the [GitHub CLI](https://cli.github.com) (`gh auth login`).
- Fixed: a low AI limit could notify again when the service adjusted its reset time.
- Corrected and refined wording in all six languages.

## Install or update

Monny 1.0.0 offers this update itself (**Check for Updates** in the menu). For a new installation, download **Monny-1.1.0-63.dmg** below, open it and drag Monny into Applications. The app is not notarized by Apple, so the first launch of a downloaded copy needs **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**; see the [installation guide](https://github.com/Blinks44/monny#install).

You can check the download against the attached **SHA256SUMS.txt**: `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## Good to know

- Requires a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later. Tested on an M1 Max with macOS 27.
- AI limits stay off by default. The Copilot reading uses an undocumented GitHub endpoint and the GitHub CLI's saved github.com account; GitHub Enterprise and other sign-ins are not supported yet.
- Power readings come from the battery gauge and differ between Mac models; a reading the Mac does not report is shown as unavailable.
