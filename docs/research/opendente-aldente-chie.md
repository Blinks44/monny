## Decision for Monny

Keep full physical verification and Discharge disabled. Prepare a separate **single fixed attempt**, with at most eight candidate reads over a two-second observation window inside the existing three-second checkpoint/watchdog budget. There is no repeated isolation setter or hysteresis loop. Restore immediately on battery/no-charge evidence, unsafe/missing telemetry, cancellation, timeout, disconnect, sleep/removal/shutdown or the bound. Restoration ignores source/cable predicates. Never call the private native getter while isolated; compare fresh actual endpoints after byte-zero rollback.

Prefer a direct separate adapter, USB-C display disconnected and lid open for the first bounded observation. This isolates the adapter-vs-dock question and avoids intentionally exercising the reported display-link failure at the same time. Hardware availability is requested from the user; no device is assumed available. Monny cannot guarantee a hard wall-clock deadline for stalled synchronous firmware/system calls; the journal/restart path and this limitation remain explicit.

Report byte rollback, battery-source observation and return-to-adapter observation independently. A zero exit requires all measured evidence, but still does not prove physical cable presence, true hardware power flow, sustained behavior or adapter-specific crash recovery. A missing transition is inconclusive and must not trigger a retry or longer experiment. BAT-025's one-pulse authorization was consumed; the new bounded command needs a concrete separate authorization after software/package preparation.
